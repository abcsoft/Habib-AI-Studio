# Habib AI Studio brand system

An editorial workspace for creative campaigns. Marketing and application share ivory backgrounds, violet primary actions, charcoal typography, thin warm-gray rules and rounded cards. Creative photography and campaign layouts carry color; interface chrome remains calm.

Use Instrument Sans for body/UI, Bricolage Grotesque for headings and Martian Mono for audit data. Serif italics punctuate marketing headings. Avoid uppercase display text, hard offset shadows, boilerplate and invented testimonials.

Tokens: canvas #faf9f6, ink #252329, violet #6d4aff, muted #726d79, border #e8e5eb, lavender #eeebff, sage #eef3e8, peach #f7e6de. Buttons are violet with white text or white with a subtle border. Cards use 12–20px radius. Focus rings stay visible. Forms have labels and errors.

Marketing: airy navigation, left-aligned hero and layered creative preview, sample creatives, workflow, features, honest plan cards, interactive demo tour, FAQ and CTA. Never imply sample campaigns or metrics are actual customer results.

Application: 232px sidebar, compact header, current credits, creation panel, summary metrics, recent campaigns and library. Mobile navigation stays accessible. Actions must work or explain service setup. Empty states lead to creation.

Respect reduced motion, keyboard interaction, screen readers, contrast and widths down to 360px. Claude authors strategy, copy and prompts; a separate image provider renders visuals. Demo is labeled and offline.
